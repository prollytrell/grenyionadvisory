Services

Nonprofit Security Basics Review (Free)

Overview

A simple, structured cybersecurity review designed to help nonprofits understand their most important risks and take practical first steps toward improving security without complexity.

What’s Included

  • Security Risk Snapshot: High-level review of your environment to identify key risks across accounts, access, data handling, and basic protections.
  • Plain-English Risk Explanations: Clear breakdown of what each risk means and why it matters to your organization.
  • Prioritized Recommendations: Actionable steps organized by urgency.
  • Policy Starter Pack: Password Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Basic Incident Response Steps
  • Walkthrough Call (30–45 min): Review findings, answer questions, and plan next steps.

Best For

  • Small nonprofits with no dedicated security team
  • Organizations unsure where to start with cybersecurity
  • Teams preparing for grants or improving operational maturity
  • Anyone looking for clarity without technical overwhelm

Pricing: Free (Limited Availability)
