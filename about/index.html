About Me

I'm a cybersecurity professional passionate about helping businesses understand and manage their risks in a practical way. My background includes various roles in security operations, advising clients, and supporting governance efforts, which gives me a broad view of how security works in everyday situations.

In my current position, I focus on detecting and responding to security incidents, using specialized tools to investigate threats and evaluate their effects. Beyond handling operations, I have helped develop company policies and procedures, prepared organizations for audits, and improved internal documentation and processes. This experience allows me to connect technical security work with governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) practices.

I work closely with clients to assess their security strengths and weaknesses and provide tailored recommendations that suit their specific business needs. My approach is simple. I strive to understand your environment, identify key concerns, and focus on practical solutions that work.

My goal is to help small businesses and growing organizations build a strong cybersecurity foundation without making things overly complicated. Whether I'm identifying risks, enhancing documentation, or aligning with industry standards, I aim to offer clear guidance that supports both security and business growth.
